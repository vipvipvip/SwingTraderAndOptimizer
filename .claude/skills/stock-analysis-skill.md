## FRAMEWORK QUICK START

**Core Thesis:** Mid-cap stocks ($5B-$50B) in early R&D product adoption phase (3-6 months into launch) provide 4-5 month window for 20-35% returns before broader market catches on.

**When to Use This Skill:**
- Analyzing any $5-50B mid-cap company for investment
- Screening for companies with new product launches
- Reading 10-Qs to find adoption proof points
- Timing entry into growth stocks
- Building a watchlist

**When NOT to Use:**
- Mega-cap companies (>$50B) — products don't move the needle
- Pre-revenue biotech — wrong adoption cycle
- Companies already up 50%+ on the thesis — edge expired
- Mature products (>18 months launched) — market already knows

## 15-POINT SCORING SYSTEM (Apply This First)

### Product & Revenue (6 points max)
- **Product named in MD&A:** 2 pts (specific name like "Search AI Platform" or "Connected Frontline")
- **Revenue itemized:** 2 pts (separate line item or segment breakdown showing product contribution)
- **Growth >15% YoY:** 2 pts (revenue or cRPO/RPO growth >15%, proves adoption is real)

### Customer & Market Proof (4 points max)
- **Customer names disclosed:** 2 pts (e.g., "600+ customers using AI" or specific ACV cohorts)
- **Guidance includes product:** 2 pts (product mentioned in forward-looking guidance, not just press release)

### Operational Proof (3 points max)
- **RPO/Backlog disclosed:** 1 pt (RPO or cRPO growth >12% YoY = customer commitment)
- **Risk factors mention product:** 1 pt (if material, appears in Item 1A)
- **Profitability proof:** 1 pt (margins stable/expanding despite growth = real adoption, not cost cuts)

### Management Quantification (2 points max)
- **Earnings call quantifies adoption:** 1 pt (specific numbers: adoption rates, customer counts, revenue contribution)
- **Operational metrics disclosed:** 1 pt (e.g., feature penetration %, implementation velocity, usage rates)

**Total Possible: 15 points**
