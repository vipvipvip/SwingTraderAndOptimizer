# Sources & Checklist — Private Companies, Startups, Advisory Boards

Data is thin; be explicit about what is unknown and keep inferred conclusions labeled as inferred.

## Where to look
- Company website — leadership, board, advisors, investors.
- Press releases and news — funding rounds, acquisitions, executive hires, layoffs.
- Crunchbase / PitchBook summaries (via search) — funding stage, investors (record as secondary).
- LinkedIn — leadership tenure, headcount trend, board/advisor members (Max can check directly).
- Florida Division of Corporations (sunbiz.org) for Florida entities — officers and registered agents.
- Local ecosystem coverage: Orlando Business Journal, Orlando Inc., Synapse, StarterStudio, Florida venture coverage.

## Private-company questions
- Stage and funding: bootstrapped, VC-backed (which firms hold board seats?), PE-backed, family-owned?
- Is there a formal board, or only an advisory board? Who controls seats (investors vs. founders)?
- Recent inflection points: new round, new CEO, pivot, acquisition interest, preparing for exit?
- What expertise do founders lack that an outside director or advisor would supply?
