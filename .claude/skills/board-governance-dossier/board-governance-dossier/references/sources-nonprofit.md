# Sources & Checklist — Nonprofits

## Where to look
- **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer** (projects.propublica.org/nonprofits) — free full Form 990s, multi-year. Best primary source.
- **Candid / GuideStar** — profile, sometimes board list and audited financials.
- **Charity Navigator** — ratings (Accountability & Finance, Impact). Ratings change; record the date viewed.
- **Organization website** — Board/Leadership page, annual report, audited financial statements.
- **Local news** — Orlando Business Journal, Orlando Sentinel, WMFE, local TV; search "[org] board", "[org] CEO", "[org] expansion".
- Florida Division of Corporations (sunbiz.org) — officers/directors on annual report filings.

## Form 990 map
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Total revenue / total expenses | Part I, Lines 12 and 18 |
| Net assets | Part I, Line 22 (and Part X) |
| Revenue mix (contributions, government grants, program service revenue) | Part VIII |
| Program vs. management vs. fundraising expenses | Part IX, Line 25, Columns B, C, D vs. A (program ratio = B ÷ A; 75%+ is typically considered efficient) |
| Voting members / independent members | Part VI, Section A, Lines 1a and 1b |
| Governance policies (conflict of interest, whistleblower, document retention) | Part VI, Section B |
| Officers, directors, key employees, compensation | Part VII, Section A |
| Mission and major programs | Part III |
| Financial assistance / charity care (hospitals) | Schedule H; other assistance may appear in Part III narratives |

Note: 990s lag 1–2 years. Always record the fiscal year and check whether a newer filing exists.

## Nonprofit-specific questions
- Structural surplus or deficit, and is it a trend?
- Dependence on one funder type (especially government grants)?
- Months of operating reserves (net assets ÷ monthly expenses, roughly)?
- ED/CEO tenure; any recent turnover?
- Missing governance policies in Part VI Section B (a quiet red flag)?
- Is the board large and donor-heavy (a "giving board") or small and skills-based? This tells Max what the seat will really expect — fundraising obligations ("give or get") are common and worth asking about.
