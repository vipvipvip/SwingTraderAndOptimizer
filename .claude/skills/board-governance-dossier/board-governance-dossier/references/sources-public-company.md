# Sources & Checklist — Public Companies

## Where to look
- **SEC EDGAR** (sec.gov/edgar) — primary filings.
  - **DEF 14A (proxy statement):** director bios, board skills matrix, committees, independence, tenure, compensation, governance policies, shareholder proposals.
  - **10-K:** business overview, risk factors (look for cybersecurity — Item 1C — and technology risk), financials, MD&A.
  - **10-Q:** latest quarter.
  - **8-K:** director departures/appointments (Item 5.02), material events.
- Investor relations site — governance page, committee charters, board diversity/skills.
- Earnings call transcripts and recent news for strategic tension.

## Public-company questions
- Revenue and margin trend (3 years); debt and cash position.
- Board skills matrix: is technology, cybersecurity, or M&A expertise thin or absent?
- Board size, independence, average tenure, mandatory retirement age, upcoming retirements (open seats).
- Recent director departures (8-K Item 5.02) — a vacancy is the clearest entry point.
- Activist investor involvement or contested proxy?
- Cyber governance disclosure (10-K Item 1C): who oversees it — full board, audit committee, or a dedicated committee?

## Reality check for fit
Public-company boards usually recruit through search firms and favor prior public-board or sitting-executive experience. Be honest in the fit rating: for Max, a public board is typically a later-stage goal; an advisory board or a private/PE-backed company board is often the nearer step. Say so if it applies.
