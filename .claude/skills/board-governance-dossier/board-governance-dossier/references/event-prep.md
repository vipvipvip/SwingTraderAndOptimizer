# Event Prep Notes

Add an Event prep note to the dossier when Max is attending a specific event.

## Checklist
1. Identify hosts: ED/CEO, board chair, event chair/honorees. Note one or two people he should aim to meet and why (tie to the gap in Section 2).
2. Scan the last 30–60 days of local news (Orlando Business Journal, Orlando Sentinel) for regional pressures relevant to the organization's sector.
3. Confirm the dress code on the host's event page; if none stated, use the defaults below.
4. Have a LinkedIn QR code ready for contact sharing.

## Dress code defaults (men)
- **Luncheons, summits, issues days:** business professional — navy, charcoal, or dark grey suit; white or light blue shirt; conservative tie; leather dress shoes.
- **Evening galas / fundraisers (black-tie optional or formal):** tuxedo welcome but not required; a black or deep midnight-navy suit, white shirt, dark conservative tie, polished black shoes is appropriate.

## In the room
- Lead with intent (he's exploring board service) — it turns him from inquisitor into listener.
- Listen more than talk; the dossier is so he understands the organization, not so he recites it.
- Hold specific numbers in reserve unless the conversation turns to operations.
- Within 48 hours: personalized LinkedIn connection and a short email requesting a 20-minute coffee, referencing something specific from the conversation.
