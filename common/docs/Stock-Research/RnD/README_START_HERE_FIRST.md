## KEY FRAMEWORK RULES

### ✅ DO THIS

1. **Pull SEC 10-Q first** (not press releases)
2. **Search MD&A for product name** — if not there, not material yet
3. **Extract actual revenue numbers** — quantify adoption scale
4. **Check operating margins** — must expand, not compress
5. **Identify next catalyst** — when score will change
6. **Compare to BDX/ZBRA baselines** — where is stock in adoption cycle?

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS

1. **Score based on press alone** (BDX taught us this lesson)
2. **Ignore margin compression** (SMTC showed what happens)
3. **Assume mega-caps follow same timeline** ($500B+ reprice in 48 hours)
4. **Skip risk factors** (goodwill impairment is huge warning)
5. **Rely on analyst sentiment** (only 10-Q facts count)
