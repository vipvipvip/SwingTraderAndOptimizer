## FRAMEWORK DEFINITION (15-Point Scoring System)

### Scoring Ranges
- **0-3:** AVOID (hype without adoption proof)
- **4-6:** WATCH (early adoption, 3-6 months to inflection)
- **7-10:** CANDIDATE (proven adoption, 2-4 months to inflection)
- **11-15:** HIGH-PROBABILITY (inflection imminent, 1-3 months to surge)

### Scoring Checklist (Based on SEC Filings Only)

| Criterion | Points | 10-Q Evidence Required |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| **Product named in MD&A** | 2 | "Product XYZ revenue was $XXM" in management discussion |
| **Revenue itemized by product** | 2 | Segment table or footnote showing separate line item |
| **QoQ/YoY growth >15%** | 2 | Revenue accelerating quarter-over-quarter |
| **Customer names disclosed** | 2 | Specific customer wins in 10-Q or risk factors |
| **Guidance includes product** | 2 | Forward outlook explicitly references new product |
| **Risk factors mention product** | 1 | Product-specific adoption risks disclosed (means material) |
| **Backlog/RPO disclosed** | 1 | Remaining performance obligations or pipeline visible |
| **Supporting operational metrics** | 1 | Hiring acceleration, strategic acquisition, partnerships |
| **Profitability proof** | 1 | Margins stable/expanding despite growth (operational leverage) |
| **Earnings call commentary** | 1 | Management quantifies adoption, customer wins, pipeline |

**CRITICAL RULE:** Only count evidence from 10-Q filings. Press releases are verification only.

### Red Flags (Subtract Points)

- Revenue declining while product hyped → -2 pts
- Governance issues (auditor changes, executive scandal) → -1 pt
- Major gap: press claims ≠ 10-Q reality → -2 pts
- Goodwill impairment >5% of market cap → -2 pts
- Pre-revenue with vague timeline (2+ years) → -1 pt
- Margin compression despite revenue growth → -1 pt

## INVESTMENT THESIS SUMMARY

### The Framework Works Because:

1. **Press ≠ 10-Q:** Markets rally on announcements, but 10-Q shows if adoption is real
2. **Timing Edge:** By the time earnings confirm adoption, stock has already moved 5-10%
3. **Inflection Predictable:** Once adoption visible in 10-Q, next 2-4 quarters show acceleration
4. **Margin Expansion:** True R&D adoption improves operational efficiency (not BDX yet, but ZBRA shows this)

### Why Mid-Caps ($5B-$50B)?

- Large enough to have resources for real R&D
- Small enough that single product can move needle (unlike mega-caps)
- Not mega-caps where market reprices within 48 hours

### Why SEC Filings First?

- Press can lie; 10-Q is audited (legal consequences)
- Segment revenue breakouts show adoption velocity
- Risk factors show if company sees risk as material
- Forward guidance shows management confidence
