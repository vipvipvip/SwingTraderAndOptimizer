MTF Daily Signal (daily_signal_service.py): BUY signal if weekly EMA(10) > SMA(40) AND daily EMA(10) > SMA(40) AND 1-hour EMA just crossed above SMA(40). Scored by momentum + gap + infancy. Signal-only via Slack. (Multi-TF-EMAC Scan)

	gap_w = (close_weekly - sma40_weekly) / sma40_weekly * 100
	How far price is stretched above its 40-week SMA, as a percentage. High gap_w means the stock has run up far from its cost basis — signals strong momentum but also risk of mean reversion. Scored as min(gap_w / 20, 3) — caps at 3 pts when 60%+ above SMA40.

	atr_dist = (close_hourly - atr_stop_hourly) / close_hourly * 100
	Distance from the ATR trailing stop on the hourly chart, as a percentage. atr_stop is close - sma(tr, 14) * 2.0 — a 2x average true range band below price. Wide atr_dist means the stock has room before hitting its trailing stop. Scored as min(atr_dist / 1.5, 3) — caps at 3 pts when 4.5%+ above the stop.
	Both are capped at 3 pts each, plus fresh_pts (0-2), giving a max MTF score of 8.

	Testing with low score but with all greens. However:
	Not good. -40.77%, 49% win rate, 45.9% max DD. The Early score kills returns.
	The reason: penalizing gap_w removes the very stocks that produce explosive gains. The original finding was right — high gap_w stocks (WDC +1,517%, APP +741%) kept running despite being "stretched." The gap widens further in strong trends before reversing. Avoiding them misses the breakout entirely.
	The MTF score's strength is that gap_w rewards momentum rather than penalizing it. The key insight from the backtest still holds: you want stocks that have already broken out hard and are still running, not stocks that just barely crossed.
