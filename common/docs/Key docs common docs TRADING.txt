	gap_w = (close_weekly - sma40_weekly) / sma40_weekly * 100
	How far price is stretched above its 40-week SMA, as a percentage. High gap_w means the stock has run up far from its cost basis — signals strong momentum but also risk of mean reversion. Scored as min(gap_w / 20, 3) — caps at 3 pts when 60%+ above SMA40.

	atr_dist = (close_hourly - atr_stop_hourly) / close_hourly * 100
	Distance from the ATR trailing stop on the hourly chart, as a percentage. atr_stop is close - sma(tr, 14) * 2.0 — a 2x average true range band below price. Wide atr_dist means the stock has room before hitting its trailing stop. Scored as min(atr_dist / 1.5, 3) — caps at 3 pts when 4.5%+ above the stop.
	Both are capped at 3 pts each, plus fresh_pts (0-2), giving a max MTF score of 8.

	Testing with low score but with all greens. However:

	The reason: penalizing gap_w removes the very stocks that produce explosive gains. The original finding was right — high gap_w stocks (WDC +1,517%, APP +741%) kept running despite being "stretched." 	The MTF score's strength is that gap_w rewards momentum rather than penalizing it. The key insight from the backtest still holds: you want stocks that have already broken out hard and are still running, not stocks that just barely crossed.

====== Top 10 ETF selection explanation
Yes — scores update daily with fresh prices. Three things drive changes:
1. Hard filters kill entries — if VTI's hourly close drops below its ATR stop, or weekly EMA crosses below SMA, it gets filtered out entirely (line 156-158). Something like XLU or TLT replaces it.
2. Freshness decays — fresh_pts = max(0, 2 - days_since/60). AETF that crossed 60+ days ago loses all freshness points and drops in rank.
3. gap_w and atr_dist fluctuate — as prices move, the distance from SMA40 and ATR stop changes daily.
But realistically with 22 ETFs and 10 slots, turnover is slow. The biggest driver will be regime shifts — if broad market tanks, defensive ETFs (XLU, TLT, GLD) surge into the top-10 while growth ETFs (QQQ, VGT, SMH) drop out.
Right now all 22 have atr_dist = 0 for most because they're ETFs — they don't gap as aggressively as individual stocks. The scores are dominated by gap_w (distance from weekly SMA40) and freshness. XLF leads at 1.9 because it's the freshest crossover (28 days) with decent gap.
