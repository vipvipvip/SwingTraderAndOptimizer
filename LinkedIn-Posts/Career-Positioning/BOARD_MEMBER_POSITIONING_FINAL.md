### ABOUT SECTION

**My boardroom philosophy: eyes in, hands out.**

I'm not a networker with a Rolodex or an advisor who recommends and walks away. I'm the navigator who reads the conditions honestly and calls the course correction, while you keep both hands on the wheel.

My approach to governance is collaborative yet unvarnished: I dig into the raw data to anticipate risk, respect operational boundaries, and partner with executive teams on long-term strategy.

I help visionary founders and C-suites run their most critical operations. Not advisory—true partnership, sweat equity and owner mindset - a true force multiplier.

**What I do:**

**For startups:** I run tech and product strategy built for enterprise scale from day one — not scrambling to retrofit compliance and security when you're closing a large deal. For everything else, I'm your sounding board — providing guardrails, direction, and leading conversations to find solutions.

**For growth-stage/IPO-ready companies:** I focus deeply on one critical strategic function — pressure-testing the bets and holding management accountable for the ones that move the needle.

**For large enterprises:** Bring entrepreneurial operator mentality to established boards. Rigorous enterprise-level tech understanding + willingness to challenge status quo.

**How I work:**

**1. I see what's hard to see from inside**
Leadership teams are often too close to their own blind spots. I read the conditions honestly, flag the real bottleneck, and hold the team accountable for closing it — that's the board's job: see clearly, not execute.

**2. I challenge, not validate**
If you're wrong, I'll tell you. If you're right but thinking too small, I'll push you. Boards that only nod aren't boards — they're audiences.

**3. I bring pattern recognition, not war stories**
Years building and scaling companies taught me what separates real product-market fit from good execution chasing the wrong market. I bring that judgment to the table — not to relive it, but to help you avoid repeating it.

**4. My incentives are aligned with yours, not detached from them**
I hold equity, not just an opinion. If the strategy fails, I feel it too. That alignment is what separates a true partner from an advisor who collects a fee and moves on.

**5. I hold rigor over rhetoric**
I ask for the data behind the confidence. I measure what matters, not what's convenient to report. If something isn't working, I'll say so immediately — no political softening.

**I work with:**
- Founders playing the long game — building something durable, not flipping for a quick multiple
- Teams that earn trust the hard way (real client relationships, real revenue) rather than manufacture it
- Leaders who take care of the people who build the company with them
- Teams where tech/operations are core to the bet, not an afterthought

**How I engage:** Open to board seats and select fractional operating roles.
