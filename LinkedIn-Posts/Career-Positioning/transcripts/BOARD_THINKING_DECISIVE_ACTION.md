# Board-Level Thinking: Seeing the Signal and Acting Decisively

## The Lesson from Censeo

**Internal note for board conversations and positioning**

### The Pattern Recognition Story

By Year 3-4 at Censeo, the customer data made one thing clear: **Enterprise customers were fundamentally more profitable than SMBs.**

The economics were stark:
- Enterprise: Longer contracts (3-5 years), higher ACV ($100K+), high retention, expansion revenue, minimal hand-holding
- SMB: Shorter contracts (1 year), lower ACV ($10-30K), churn risk, no expansion, sales-intensive

A founder could see this data and do nothing (keep hedging, keep serving both). Or a founder could see it and act decisively (focus enterprise exclusively, reallocate all resources).

**I hedged for 2-3 years.** Kept saying "both segments." Kept hiring reps trained on SMB close tactics. Kept building for low-value customers. Revenue grew anyway, so the cost wasn't immediately obvious.

But the opportunity cost was real: We could've hit $2.5M profitability 2-3 years faster by focusing earlier.

### Why This Is Board-Level Thinking

**Boards care about this specific skill: Seeing patterns in data and making decisive bets, not hedging.**

At the board level, there are three types of operators:

1. **Data-blind operators** — Build intuitively, don't track signals, get lucky or fail
2. **Data-aware but indecisive operators** — See the patterns but keep hedging, stay comfortable, grow slowly
3. **Data-driven and decisive operators** — See the pattern, make the call, reallocate everything, execute

Censeo's mistake was being #2 for 2-3 years. We had #3 operators could've cut years off our path to scale.

### The Board Question

When a board member asks "What data are you NOT acting on?", they're testing whether you're decisive. The answer matters more than the data itself.

Bad answer: "We're tracking CAC and LTV and it shows SMBs are lower margin, so we're thinking about focusing more on enterprise."

Good answer: "We tracked CAC and LTV by Year 3. Enterprise is 5x more profitable. By Year 4, we stopped chasing SMBs entirely. Here's how we reallocated: hired enterprise reps, built for their compliance needs, changed our GTM."

### Talking Point for Board Conversations

**"I learned the difference between data-awareness and decisiveness. You can have the best metrics in the world, but if you hedge and don't act, you're leaving money on the table. At Censeo, we saw the enterprise signal by Year 3-4. We didn't move decisively until Year 4-5. That cost us 2 years. Now when I see a clear pattern in the data, I move. No hedging."**

### Related Skills This Tests

- **Pattern recognition** — Can you see signals in customer data?
- **Decisiveness** — Can you make a bet and stick with it?
- **Willingness to eliminate optionality** — Can you say "no" to the less profitable path?
- **Resource reallocation** — Can you move all your chips to the winning segment?

These are the exact skills boards want in operators who can spot bottlenecks and fix them.
