## Questions to Ask When Evaluating Founders

Before saying yes to an advisory engagement:

1. **Do they have technical depth?** (Can they appreciate pattern recognition)
2. **Is there a specific problem?** (Not just "advisory board sounds good")
3. **Is there a timeline?** (When do they need input? How frequently?)
4. **Is there upside?** (Equity component? Board progression path?)
5. **Do they iterate?** (Or do they want a 6-month strategy handed over?)
